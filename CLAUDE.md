# Eastman Film Lab signage

This repo holds sign designs for Eastman Film Lab.

**Before designing anything, read `BRAND.md`.** Color and font consistency is the top priority.
- Use only the palette in `BRAND.md` / `assets/brand/tokens.css`. The brand orange is `#F6853D`. Secondary tones are opacity tints of orange or black. Do not introduce new hues.
- Wordmark: Helvetica Neue Bold with tight tracking. Korean text and prices: Pretendard.
- Reference originals are in `assets/reference/`, and exported crops (panels, QR, swatches) are in `assets/exports/`.
